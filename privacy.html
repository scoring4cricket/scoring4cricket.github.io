Scoring 4 Cricket: Privacy policy

Scoring 4 Cricket (scoring4cricket.github.io) is a free scoring app for club cricket.

What we collect

When a scorer signs in with Google, we receive their name, email address and profile picture from Google. We use these only to identify the scorer and to let the app owner approve or block scoring access. Scorers may also give their club or team name.

The match data scorers enter, such as team names, player names and ball-by-ball scores, is saved so matches can be resumed and shown on live scoreboards.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a Google Sheet owned by the app owner and on the scorer's own device. We do not sell or share data with anyone else, and we do not use advertising or tracking.

Public live links

Anyone with a match's live link can view that match's scoreboard. Scorers can choose to show players' full names, first names only, or initials only, for example for junior matches.

Removing data

To have your account or match data removed, email scoring4cricket@gmail.com.
