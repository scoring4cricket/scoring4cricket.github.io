1. Put the app on your home screen

  • iPhone / iPad: open scoring4cricket.github.io in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open it in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • Laptop: just use it in your browser.

Always open the app from the home-screen icon on match day. It keeps working without signal and syncs when you're back online.

2. Sign in and get approved

  1. Tap Continue with Google and pick your Google account.
  2. Enter your name and club, then tap Send request.
  3. The app owner gets an email and approves you. Tap Check again and you're in.

Spectators don't need to sign in. They just open the live link you send them.

3. Squads and Teams

  • On Home, open Squads and Teams, type a team name and tap Create.
  • Tap the team to add players, set left-handed batters (LHB) and add a logo.
  • Everyone can see and use every team. Only the person who made a team, or the owner, can change it.

4. Start a match

  1. Tap + Start a new match, then select Team 1 and Team 2.
  2. Add the date and ground. Tap More for competition, round and umpires.
  3. Choose the Competition type (One Day, T10, T20, 2 Day or SMCA Winter Comp). The overs and rules fill in for you.
  4. Pick the Playing XI for each side, with captain and wicketkeeper, then the toss and the openers.

Junior match? Set Names on the public link to First names or Initials. The app does this for you when a team name includes an age group like U13.

5. Scoring

  • Tap 0–6 for runs off the bat. Use Wide, No ball, Bye and Leg bye for extras.
  • WICKET walks you through how they got out, who was out and the fielder.
  • Undo takes back the last ball. You can also edit any ball later from Scorecard → Ball by Ball.
  • At the end of each over, pick the next bowler and tap End over.
  • Actions has switch batters, change wicketkeeper, revise target (including DLS), breaks in play, end innings and end match.

6. Share the live score

  • Tap Public link under the keypad and send it on WhatsApp. People watch the score update live.
  • On Home, Share → Live Score lists every match being scored right now.
  • Share → Scorer gives you the link to invite another scorer.

7. After the match

  • Share scorecard makes an image or a PDF, or copies text for WhatsApp.
  • Every match is kept in Completed Matches, on any device you sign in on.

Good to know

  • One scorer per match. Don't score the same match on two phones at once.
  • The dot next to your initials shows saving: green = saved, amber = saving, red = offline (it will sync later).
  • Something wrong? Use Report a problem in Admin or More.
